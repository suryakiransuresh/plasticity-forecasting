**Held-out-family limitation and OOD-aware fallback (controlled GPT-2; 40 held-out episodes).** Acquisition forecasting remains reasonably strong under family shift (overall (R^2=0.727); per-family (R^2=0.595)–(0.810)). Damage forecasting is substantially less robust: its overall held-out-family (R^2=-0.168), with strongly negative values for facts, symbols, and rules. Arithmetic is the hardest held-out family: frozen forecasts average (D=0.00349), whereas realized damage averages (D=0.08229) (23.6× underprediction), and 9/10 episodes exceed the damage budget. An OOD-aware rule flags 34/40 episodes and falls back predominantly to Late (5\times10^{-6}), recovering zero observed violations ((G=3.879), (D=0.00070)) at substantial utility cost relative to static and forecasted selection. These results show a controlled limitation under distribution shift, not universal failure of damage forecasting.
